Privacy Policy

App: SalaHulddin Azkar
Developer: Ahmed Salahulddin
Last updated: September 3, 2026

1. Introduction

SalaHulddin Azkar is an Islamic remembrance (Azkar) application. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and your rights.

2. Data We Collect

SalaHulddin Azkar does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Specifically:

  • Location: Not collected. The app never requests or accesses your location.
  • Personal identifiers: Not collected.
  • Usage data: Not collected or sent to any server.
  • Device data: Not collected or stored outside the device.

All app settings and data (such as favorite Azkar and notification preferences) are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted.

3. Notifications

The app may send local push notifications to remind you of Azkar at scheduled times. These notifications are generated entirely on your device and do not involve any external server or third-party service. You can disable notifications at any time from your device settings.

4. Third-Party Services

SalaHulddin Azkar does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
